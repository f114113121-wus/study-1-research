Survey: AI Care Assistant Language Style and Older Adults’ Sense of Companionship

Dear Participant,

Thank you for participating in this study. This research explores how an "AI care assistant's" language style influences older adults’ sense of companionship through empathic accuracy and perceived caregiving fit, while considering individual differences in social interaction needs. Before answering the questionnaire, you will first watch a 2-minute video showing interactions between an AI care assistant and an older user. After viewing the video, please respond based on your actual feelings. The survey will take approximately 5–10 minutes.

This study collects data anonymously and is for academic purposes only. No personal identifiable information will be collected, and all results will be reported in aggregate form. Participation is voluntary, and you may withdraw at any time. There are no right or wrong answers, please answer honestly based on your experience. If you have any questions, please feel free to contact the research team.

Thank you for your participation, and we wish you good health and well-being.
